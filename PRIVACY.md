# Privacy Policy for FreYo

FreYo is built as an Open Source app. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
FreYo does NOT collect, store, or share any personal information. 
- No account registration is required.
- No tracking or analytics are implemented.
- Your search history and subscriptions are stored locally on your device and are never sent to any server.

### 2. Third-Party Services
Since FreYo is a client for YouTube, it may interact with YouTube's servers to fetch data. However, this app does not use Google Play Services or any proprietary Google APIs.

### 3. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@mbahdodoy.my.id.
